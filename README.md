## About me

I am a **Software Engineer from Brazil** with a strong interest in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, MLOps, Data Science, Computer Vision, Edge AI and Telecom systems**.

My work combines software engineering, applied AI and data-driven solutions, with experience in projects involving **deep learning, model optimization, computer vision, time series analysis, dashboards, APIs, automation and cloud-based systems**.

I enjoy building practical solutions that connect research, engineering and real-world applications.
